# 📖 精读报告：The carbon price politicians dare not mention（碳定价社论）

## 一、3 个黄金功能句

### 1️⃣ 【功能标签】#否定重构 #先破后立
> 【原句摘抄】The problem is not that green technology is too expensive; it is that fossil fuels remain too cheap.

【入选理由】"not that A; it is that B" 先否定流行误解、再把真因推上前台，是 Task 2 驳论段最能体现论证层次的高分骨架。
【仿写示范】The problem is not that students spend too much time online; it is that schools fail to teach them how to use it productively.（保留 not...; it is that... 骨架，话题迁移到教育）

### 2️⃣ 【功能标签】#驳斥 #因果
> 【原句摘抄】Critics warn that carbon taxes would cripple growth and punish the poor. The evidence suggests otherwise — and where the burden does bite, the answer is to recycle the revenue as dividends, not to abandon the instrument altogether.

【入选理由】先摆出对方观点再一句驳斥（suggests otherwise），并顺势给出建设性替代方案，正是 Task 2 让步反驳段的满分节奏。
【仿写示范】Critics warn that banning private cars would cripple urban economies. The evidence suggests otherwise — and where the restrictions do bite, the answer is to improve public transport, not to abandon the policy altogether.（将 carbon taxes 替换为 banning private cars，revenue 替换为 public transport，话题迁移到环境/交通）

### 3️⃣ 【功能标签】#让步 #强调
> 【原句摘抄】Even if every country honoured its current pledges in full, the world would still warm by around 2.1°C; pricing carbon is therefore not radical but the moderate minimum.

【入选理由】"Even if + 最理想假设 + 仍然不够" 的虚拟让步一击证明措施必要性；结尾 "not A but B" 收紧立场，是收尾段利器。
【仿写示范】Even if every student revised for hours every day, exam anxiety would still affect their performance; learning to manage stress is therefore not a luxury but a basic skill.（ pledges/2.1°C 抽象化为 exam anxiety，虚拟让步骨架原样保留，话题迁移到教育）

## 二、核心词汇链

📘 **a price on carbon / carbon pricing**
【词性+中文意义】名词短语；碳定价
【入选理由】环境类 Task 2 核心概念，比 carbon tax 更书面、更宏观，正文首尾两次出现。
【雅思迁移句】Carbon pricing is widely seen as the most cost-effective way to curb emissions.

📘 **cripple growth**
【词性+中文意义】动词搭配；严重阻碍（经济）增长
【入选理由】cripple 是书面语中"严重损害"的高分替换词，与 growth / economy 搭配常见于考官范文。
【雅思迁移句】Critics argue that strict environmental regulations would cripple economic growth.

📘 **recycle the revenue**
【词性+中文意义】动词短语；（把税收）以分红等形式返还民众
【入选理由】政策讨论中"税收返还"的地道说法，政府/环境话题可直接复用。
【雅思迁移句】Governments could recycle the revenue from green taxes by cutting income tax for low earners.

🗣️ **bite**（where the burden does bite）
【词性+中文意义】动词；（政策/负担）真正产生痛感、开始起作用
【入选理由】口语中形容"政策真疼了"非常地道，Part 3 讨论政策效果时能明显提升自然度。
【雅思迁移句】Austerity policies really bite when they start affecting everyday services.

⚖️ **emissions per head**
【词性+中文意义】名词短语；人均排放量
【入选理由】环境数据讨论的精准书面表达，per head 比 per person 更正式，适用环境/社会类话题。
【雅思迁移句】Emissions per head in developed countries remain far higher than the global average.

## 三、精读指导与避坑指南

- **宏观骨架**：社论立场为强烈赞同碳定价；论证走向是"数据立论 → 否定重构指出真因 → 驳斥反对派并给替代方案 → 虚拟让步强化必要性 → 呼吁行动"的先立后破结构。
- **难度对比**：词汇 7.5（cripple / recycle the revenue / bite 等地道搭配密集）；句式 7.5（分号长句、破折号插入语、虚拟让步从句）；逻辑 8（立论-驳斥-让步层层推进，可直接当作 Task 2 结构范本）。
- **避坑提示**：
  1. 第三段 "Even if every country honoured its pledges" 是虚拟语气，honoured 不是拼写错误，别误判为一般过去时事实陈述。
  2. "the instrument" 跨句回指 carbon pricing；读到指代词时要回找逻辑链，不要逐词硬译。
